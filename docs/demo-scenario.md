# Demo Scenario: Crypto Strategy Lab

This document outlines a structured step-by-step walkthrough for demonstrating the MVP capabilities of the Crypto Strategy Lab during the final seminar.

## Setup & Initialization

### Expected Action:
1. Start the PostgreSQL (or SQLite) database.
2. Launch the backend: `uvicorn src.main:app --reload`
3. Launch the frontend: `npm run dev`
4. Open the browser to `http://localhost:5173`.

### Expected Result:
The application loads instantly. The Dashboard displays the navigation bar with active styling, and the layout collapses perfectly on mobile viewport simulations.

---

## Step 1: Real-time Multi-timeframe Visualization (Dashboard)

### Expected Action:
1. Navigate to the **Dashboard**.
2. Select a pair (e.g., `BTC/USDT`) and interval (e.g., `1m` or `15m`).
3. Point out the glowing **LIVE** badge on the chart.

### Expected Result:
- Historical candles load immediately.
- The most recent candle on the right edge pulses/updates in real-time as WebSocket ticks arrive from Binance.
- System stability is highlighted: The backend multiplexes one Binance connection to multiple browser tabs seamlessly.

---

## Step 2: Running a Manual Backtest

### Expected Action:
1. Scroll down to the **Backtest Configuration** section on the Dashboard.
2. Select the `ma_crossover` strategy.
3. Configure parameters (e.g., Short MA: 10, Long MA: 50).
4. Hit **Run Backtest**.

### Expected Result:
- The UI displays a loading state.
- The `TradeDetailTable` populates with a chronological list of simulated executions, color-coding profits (green) and losses (red).
- Key metrics (Win Rate, Total Return, Max Drawdown, Sharpe Ratio, Profit Factor) calculate instantly.

---

## Step 3: Automated Strategy Search Engine

### Expected Action:
1. Navigate to the **AI Search** page (`/search`).
2. Input target parameters: `Candidates: 1000`, `Batch Size: 50`.
3. Click **START SEARCH**.

### Expected Result:
- The Progress Dashboard lights up.
- The progress bar fills dynamically, showing candidates tested vs. time elapsed.
- The `Top Discoveries` table live-updates as the engine discovers increasingly optimal parameter configurations.
- Hit **PAUSE** to demonstrate state control.

---

## Step 4: Leaderboard & Event-Driven Decoupling

### Expected Action:
1. Navigate to the **Leaderboard** (`/leaderboard`).
2. Point out that the manual backtests from Step 2 and the AI-generated strategies from Step 3 are *already* here.

### Expected Result:
- Demonstrates architectural decoupling: No module directly touched the Leaderboard. The `BACKTEST_COMPLETED` event was published to the `EventBus`, and the Leaderboard silently picked it up and updated its database in the background.

---

## Step 5: ML Sentiment & News Integration

### Expected Action:
1. Navigate to the **News Feed** (`/news`).
2. Observe the auto-fetching of live RSS feeds.
3. Look at the **Market Sentiment** dashboard widget at the top.

### Expected Result:
- The AI Sentiment Gauge displays a score out of 100, representing the fear/greed generated by reading the news.
- The progress bars show the exact Distribution percentage (Positive vs Negative).
- Each news article card displays a color-coded badge (`POSITIVE`, `NEGATIVE`, `NEUTRAL`) processed by the NLP fallback/Transformers pipeline. 
- Highlight the `news_sentiment_strategy` that can trade directly off these dynamic metrics.
